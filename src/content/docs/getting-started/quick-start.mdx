This guide walks you through creating your first TinyFat agent.

## Prerequisites

- An email address
- An [Anthropic API key](https://console.anthropic.com/) (Claude)

<Aside type="tip">
  Don't have an Anthropic key yet? Sign up at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com/). New accounts get free credits.
</Aside>

## Create your agent

Follow these steps to get started:

<Steps>

1. **Sign up at [tinyfat.com/signup](https://tinyfat.com/signup)**
   
   Enter your email address. We'll send you a magic link.

2. **Click the magic link**
   
   Check your inbox (and spam folder). Click the link to continue.

3. **Choose your agent name**
   
   Pick a name for your agent. This becomes part of its email address.
   
   - **Free tier:** `{name}-{hash}@tinyfat.com` (e.g., `buddy-a1b2c3d4@tinyfat.com`)
   - **Tiny tier ($5/mo):** Custom alias like `buddy@tinyfat.com`

4. **Add your Anthropic API key**
   
   Paste your API key. It's encrypted and stored securely.
   
   <Aside type="note">
     We never see your key in plain text. It's encrypted before storage and only decrypted inside your agent's container.
   </Aside>

5. **You're ready!**
   
   Your agent is now live and waiting for email.

</Steps>

## Send your first email

Open your email client and send a message:

**To:** `yourname@tinyfat.com` (the address from step 3)  
**Subject:** `Hello!`  
**Body:** `Hi there! What can you help me with?`

You'll get a response in 10-30 seconds.

## What just happened?

1. Resend received your email and notified TinyFat
2. TinyFat validated that you're an allowed sender
3. A container started with your agent's storage mounted
4. Your agent read your message, thought about it, and wrote a response
5. TinyFat sent the response back to you

## Upgrading to Tiny tier

The free tier is great for trying things out. Upgrade to Tiny ($5/month) for:

- **Custom email alias** — `yourname@tinyfat.com` instead of `yourname-abc123@tinyfat.com`
- **Slack integration** — Real-time chat in your workspace
- **Priority support** — Faster response to questions

Upgrade in the dashboard under Settings → Billing.
