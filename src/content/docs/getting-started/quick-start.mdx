This guide walks you through creating your first TinyFat agent.

## Prerequisites

- An email address

That's it. No API keys required — we provide $1 of free usage to get started.

## Create your agent

<Steps>

1. **Sign up at [tinyfat.com/signup](https://tinyfat.com/signup)**
   
   Enter your email address. We'll send you a magic link.

2. **Click the magic link**
   
   Check your inbox (and spam folder). Click the link to continue.

3. **Choose your agent name**
   
   Pick a name for your agent. This becomes part of its email address:
   `{name}-{hash}@tinyfat.com` (e.g., `buddy-a1b2c3d4@tinyfat.com`)

4. **You're ready!**
   
   Your agent is live with $1 of free credits. Start emailing!

</Steps>

## Send your first email

Open your email client and send a message:

**To:** `yourname@tinyfat.com` (the address from step 3)  
**Subject:** `Hello!`  
**Body:** `Hi there! What can you help me with?`

You'll get a response in 10-30 seconds.

## What just happened?

1. Resend received your email and notified TinyFat
2. TinyFat validated that you're an allowed sender
3. A container started with your agent's storage mounted
4. Your agent read your message, thought about it, and wrote a response
5. TinyFat sent the response back to you

## Free tier

You get **$1 of API credits** free — enough for dozens of conversations with Haiku (the default model).

When your credits run out, you can:

1. **Upgrade to Tiny ($5/month)** — Includes metered API usage at cost
2. **Bring your own key (BYOK)** — Use your Anthropic API key directly
