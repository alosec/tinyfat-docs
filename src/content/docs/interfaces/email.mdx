Email is the default interface. No apps to install, works from any device.

## Your agent's address

Every agent gets: `yourname@tinyfat.com`

This is a real email address. Send from Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, your phone — anywhere.

## How it works

```
You send email → Agent wakes up → Agent thinks → Agent replies
```

1. **You email** `yourname@tinyfat.com`
2. **Resend** receives it, triggers webhook
3. **Platform** validates sender, wakes container
4. **Agent** processes your message
5. **Agent** writes response to outbox
6. **Platform** sends the reply
7. **You** get the email

Cold start is 30-60 seconds. Warm containers respond in 10-30 seconds.

## Allowed senders

Only whitelisted emails can trigger your agent. This prevents:
- Spam burning your API credits
- Random people accessing your agent

Configure in **Dashboard → Settings → Allowed Senders**.

### Wildcards

- `user@example.com` — Exact match
- `@yourcompany.com` — Anyone at that domain

<Aside type="caution">
  Domain wildcards are powerful. `@gmail.com` would let anyone with Gmail message your agent.
</Aside>

## Threading

Replies include proper email headers (`In-Reply-To`, `References`). Threads work correctly in your email client.

Use your email app's Reply button to maintain conversation threads.

## Attachments

Text attachments work. Large or binary files may be truncated.

## When to use email

- ✅ Async tasks that don't need immediate response
- ✅ When you're away from a computer
- ✅ Delegating work you'll check on later
- ✅ Sending from mobile
